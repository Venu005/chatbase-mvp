Product guide · 25 September 2026

Chatbase India

An AI support assistant for Indian small businesses. You train it on your website, PDFs and FAQs. It answers customers on your website and on WhatsApp, in Hindi, Hinglish, English and other Indian languages. When it can't answer, a person from your team takes over.

2 channels: website chat, WhatsApp 4 AI providers: OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, Sarvam, offline mock 4 plans billed in INR through Razorpay 125 automated checks (21 unit, 104 end-to-end)

Getting started

A business owner's first day

  1. Sign upE-mail and password. The Free plan gives 1 agent and 50 answers a month.
  2. Create an agentGive it a name. In Settings, add instructions, a welcome message and your brand colour.
  3. Add knowledgeSources tab: your website, a price list PDF, or a pasted FAQ.
  4. Test itPlayground tab: ask what customers ask. Correct weak answers in the Q&A tab.
  5. Go liveEmbed tab: paste one script tag into your site. Connect WhatsApp if you use it.
  6. Improve weeklyAnswer customers who asked for a person in Chats, download leads, and use Analytics to fill knowledge gaps.
Part 1

Teach the assistant

Knowledge sources Retry is new

Where: Agent → Sources

The assistant answers only from what you give it. Each source is split into passages and indexed so the right ones are found for each question. Answers cite the passage they came from, like [1], with a link to the page.

How to use

  1. Choose Website, File or Text / FAQ.
  2. Website: paste a URL and choose how many pages to read (this page, or up to 5, 10 or 20 pages on the same site).
  3. File: upload a PDF, TXT, MD or CSV up to 10 MB.
  4. Text: give it a title (for example "Refund policy") and paste the content.
  5. Wait for the status to change from processing to ready. The count of passages shows how much was read.

Good to know

  • Up to 50 sources per agent. Removing a source makes the assistant forget it at once.
  • If a source is still processing after 20 minutes (for example because the server restarted), it is marked failed.
  • Failed website sources have a Retry button that reads the site again with the same page count. Files and pasted text have to be added again.
  • Menus, footers and scripts are stripped from web pages. Hindi text is split at the danda (।), so sentences stay whole.
  • Private or internal addresses (for example localhost or cloud metadata IPs) are refused.

Q&A answers and "Fix this answer" New · PR #2

Where: Agent → Q&A, plus a Fix this answer link under every bot answer in Chats and in Analytics

Answers you write take priority over everything in the sources. When a customer asks the same question, or one that means the same, the assistant gives your answer, in the customer's language. This is the fastest way to correct the assistant.

How to use

  1. In Chats, open a conversation and press Fix this answer under the reply you want to change.
  2. The form is filled in with the customer's question and the bot's answer. Rewrite the answer and press Save answer.
  3. The reply is marked ✓ Fixed, and the next customer who asks gets your answer.
  4. To add answers ahead of time, or to edit and delete them, use the Q&A tab.

Good to know

  • Up to 500 Q&A answers per agent. Edits take effect at once.
  • Questions are matched by meaning, not exact words. The strictness is the ANSWER_FIX_MIN_SCORE setting (default 0.5).
  • An answer from Q&A has no source link, so no unrelated citations are shown with it.
  • The playground uses your Q&A answers too, so you can check a fix right away.

Playground and settings Owner-only

Where: Agent → Playground and Agent → Settings

Settings

  • Instructions: tone, business details, what to do when the answer isn't known (for example "share our WhatsApp number").
  • Welcome message and brand colour for the chat window.
  • Talking to a person: turn handoff on or off, the message customers see, and e-mail alerts.
  • Collect leads: see Lead capture.

Playground

  • A private test chat. It never hands off to a person, and it doesn't count in Analytics.
  • Only the agent's owner, signed in to the dashboard, can use it.
  • Each answer uses one message credit, like a real chat. Use Start a new chat to test follow-up questions from scratch.
Part 2

Put it where customers are

Website chat

Where: Agent → Embed

How to use

  1. Press Copy snippet. It looks like <script src="…/widget.js" data-agent-id="…" defer></script>.
  2. Paste it before </body> on your site. It works with Shopify, WordPress, Wix and hand-built sites.
  3. A chat bubble appears in the corner. Optional attributes: data-position="left" and data-color="#0f766e".
  4. You can also share the full-page chat link (/embed/<agent id>) on social media, in QR codes or in e-mails.

What visitors get

  • Answers appear word by word as they're written, with bold, lists and links, and numbered source links.
  • The conversation is kept when they reload the page.
  • Talk to a human under the chat, 👍/👎 under each answer, and optionally a details form.

Allowed websites New

Where: Agent → Embed → Allowed websites

An agent's ID is public, so anyone could copy your snippet onto their own site and spend your message credits. Listing your websites stops that.

How to use

  1. Enter your sites, one per line, for example sharmakirana.in. Subdomains such as www. or shop. are included.
  2. Press Save websites. Leave the box empty to allow any website (the default).

Good to know

  • On other sites, the bubble doesn't appear. If the chat is forced into a frame there, it says "This chat isn't enabled for this website".
  • The full-page chat link still works everywhere.
  • Up to 20 websites. This stops other websites from showing your chat. It does not stop someone who writes their own script to call the chat API directly; the per-IP rate limit still applies to them.

WhatsApp

Where: Agent → WhatsApp

How to connect

  1. Connect with Facebook (if the platform has a Meta app set up): log in, pick or create a WhatsApp Business account and number, and you're done.
  2. Or connect manually: paste the Phone number ID, access token and app secret from Meta's developer console. Then copy the webhook URL and verify token shown in the tab into Meta's WhatsApp configuration.
  3. Send a message to the number to test it.

Good to know

  • Answers are formatted for WhatsApp: *bold*, no citation markers, and up to two source links.
  • Customers can type human, agent or "insaan se baat karni hai" to reach a person.
  • Your replies from the inbox go to the customer's WhatsApp, within WhatsApp's 24-hour reply window.
  • Every WhatsApp customer is added to Leads with their number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted. Each sender can send up to 10 messages a minute.

WhatsApp voice notes New · PR #2

Where: automatic once the server has a SARVAM_API_KEY

Many customers send voice notes instead of typing. The voice note is downloaded from WhatsApp and transcribed with Sarvam's speech-to-text, which detects Hindi, English and other Indian languages. The words are then answered as if the customer had typed them.

  • In the inbox, the customer's message shows the transcript marked 🎤, so you can read what they said.
  • If a person is handling the chat, the transcript goes to them and the assistant stays quiet.
  • Voice notes longer than about 30 seconds, or ones that can't be understood, get a polite "please type your question" reply.
  • It works whichever AI writes the answers. Set VOICE_NOTES=off to turn it off.
Part 3

Conversations

Inbox and human handoff

Where: Agent → Chats

How it works

  1. A customer presses Talk to a human, or types something like "connect me to an agent", "call me", "मुझे किसी इंसान से बात करनी है" or "manager se baat karao".
  2. They see your handoff message, and on the website they can leave a phone number or e-mail.
  3. You get an e-mail and a red waiting badge. Open the chat, type a reply, and press Send reply.
  4. Press Hand back to assistant when you're done. Take over pauses the assistant in any chat.

Good to know

  • Handoff is free. While a person is handling a chat, the assistant stays quiet and uses no credits.
  • You get at most one e-mail per conversation every 15 minutes. If nobody replies for 24 hours, the chat goes back to the assistant. Both times can be changed in the server settings.
  • The inbox shows the 100 most recent conversations, with customers waiting for a reply first. It updates on its own.
  • Each conversation shows how many answers the customer rated 👎. Bot answers can be fixed from the transcript.

👍 / 👎 on answers New · PR #2

Where: under every answer in the website chat. Results appear in Chats and Analytics.

  • Visitors click 👍 or 👎. Clicking the same button again removes the rating, and the rating is kept when they reload the page.
  • Visitors can rate only answers in their own conversation, and only the assistant's answers (not the handoff message).
  • In Analytics, answers marked 👎 are grouped by question, each with a Fix this answer button.
Part 4

Leads and analytics

Lead capture New · PR #2

Where: turn it on in Settings → Collect leads. See the results in the Leads tab.

How to use

  1. Choose when to ask: After the first answer (visitors can skip, and the skip is remembered) or Before the chat starts (required).
  2. Tick the details to ask for (name, e-mail, phone) and write the message shown above the form.
  3. Open Leads to see everyone who left details. Click their first question to open the chat.
  4. Press Download CSV to open the list in Excel or Google Sheets.

Where leads come from

  • Lead form in the website chat. Phone numbers and e-mails are checked before saving. You get an e-mail for each new lead if e-mail alerts are on.
  • WhatsApp: every customer, with their number and profile name.
  • Asked for a person: the phone or e-mail a visitor leaves during a handoff.
  • You can delete a lead (for example if a customer asks to be forgotten). The conversation stays.
  • The CSV opens correctly in Excel with Hindi names, and it's protected against spreadsheet formula tricks.

Analytics New · PR #2

Where: Agent → Analytics

Shows how the assistant is doing and what to teach it next. Pick the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Days are counted in Indian time, and your own playground tests are left out.

What it shows

  • Conversations, split by website and WhatsApp.
  • Answers by the assistant and the average time to answer.
  • Helpful: the share of rated answers marked 👍.
  • Not found in your sources: how often the assistant had nothing to go on.
  • Asked for a person, and how many replies your team sent.
  • A chart of conversations per day. Hover over a day for the exact count, or open it as a table.

How to act on it

  1. Knowledge gaps lists questions nothing in your sources answered, most asked first.
  2. Press Add answer next to one, write the answer, and it's used from then on. Answered questions stop counting as gaps.
  3. Answers marked 👎 lists what customers didn't like, grouped by question, with Fix this answer.
Part 5

Account, plans and billing

Sign up and log in with e-mail and password. Each account sees only its own agents and data.

Forgot password? New On the login page, enter your e-mail and you get a link that works once, for 60 minutes. Setting a new password signs out every other device. The form says the same thing whether or not the e-mail has an account, and sends at most 3 links an hour.

Upgrading happens on Plans & billing through Razorpay Checkout (cards, UPI Autopay). The plan changes only when Razorpay confirms payment. Cancelling keeps the paid plan until the end of the billing period. To switch plans, cancel first.

Part 6

AI and languages

  • Answers stay grounded: the assistant uses only your sources and Q&A answers. When it doesn't know, it says so and suggests contacting the business, or talking to a person.
  • Customers' own language: it replies in the language and script the customer writes in, including Hinglish in English letters.
  • Follow-up questions such as "and the price?" work, because the previous question is used in the search too.
  • Formatted answers New: bold, lists, code and links in the website chat and in the inbox.

For whoever runs the server

Go-live checklist

  • Run a long-lived Node server (pnpm build && pnpm start, Docker or a VM) with Node 22.9+, pnpm 10+ and Postgres 15+ with pgvector. Ingestion runs in the background, so serverless hosting needs a job queue first.
  • Run pnpm migrate. There are now 11 migrations; 006 to 011 were added in this round, and they're safe on existing data.
  • Set AUTH_SECRET and a separate ENCRYPTION_KEY (32+ random characters each), and back up the encryption key.
  • Set APP_URL to the public https:// address. That turns on secure cookies and is needed for WhatsApp and Razorpay webhooks.
  • Choose real AI providers: LLM_PROVIDER, LLM_MODEL, keys and EMBEDDING_PROVIDER=openai. Then run pnpm preflight --live.
  • Set SMTP_URL and EMAIL_FROM. Without them there are no password reset, handoff or new-lead e-mails.
  • Set SARVAM_API_KEY for voice notes (optional).
  • Billing: the Razorpay keys, webhook secret, and pnpm razorpay:setup for the plan IDs. WhatsApp: the Meta app values for Connect with Facebook.
  • Tune RETRIEVAL_MIN_SCORE (start near 0.25 with real embeddings) and ANSWER_FIX_MIN_SCORE (0.5) on your own customers' questions.

The full list of settings is in docs/setup.md. Deployment, backups and https are covered in docs/deployment.md.

Known limits and next steps

Known limits

  • Rate limits and background ingestion live in one server's memory. More than one server needs Redis and a job queue.
  • The visitor IP is taken from X-Forwarded-For, which is only safe behind your own proxy.
  • Connect with Facebook and voice notes were tested against fake Meta and Sarvam servers, not live accounts. Test with your own number first.
  • Sarvam's instant speech-to-text takes clips under about 30 seconds. Longer voice notes get a "please type" reply.
  • The assistant doesn't read WhatsApp images. The inbox lists 100 conversations, with no search.

Suggested next features

  • Team members who can reply from the inbox without the owner's login.
  • Automatic website re-sync (weekly), so answers follow price changes.
  • Hybrid search (meaning plus keywords) for product names and SKUs.
  • Actions: order status from Shopify or WooCommerce, appointment booking, UPI payment links.
  • WhatsApp templates, for replies after 24 hours and opt-in offers.
  • Credit top-ups, GST invoices, and data export and deletion for India's DPDP Act.
