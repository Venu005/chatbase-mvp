# Your first day

From sign-up to a live assistant in about an hour.

1. **Sign up** with your e-mail and a password. The Free plan gives you 1 agent and 50 answers a month.
2. **Create an agent** and give it a name. In **Settings**, add instructions (tone, your business details, what to do when
   it doesn't know), a welcome message and your brand colour. See [playground and settings](playground-and-settings.md).
3. **Add knowledge** in the **Sources** tab: your website, a price list PDF or spreadsheet, or a pasted FAQ. See
   [knowledge sources](sources.md).
4. **Test it** in the **Playground** tab: ask what your customers ask. Correct weak answers in the **Q&A** tab, see
   [Q&A answers](qa-answers.md).
5. **Go live**: in the **Embed** tab, paste one script tag into your website ([website chat](website-chat.md)). Connect
   [WhatsApp](whatsapp.md) if you use it, and turn on [voice](voice.md) if customers prefer to talk.
6. **Improve every week**: reply to customers who asked for a person in **Chats** ([inbox](inbox-and-handoff.md)),
   download [leads](leads.md), and use [Analytics](analytics.md) to fill the gaps in what the assistant knows.

> **Tip.** The fastest way to make the assistant better is to read real conversations in **Chats** and press
> **Fix this answer** under anything it got wrong.
