# Inbox and human handoff

**Where:** Agent → **Chats**

## How handoff works

1. A customer presses **Talk to a human**, or types something like "connect me to an agent", "call me",
   "मुझे किसी इंसान से बात करनी है" or "manager se baat karao".
2. They see your handoff message, and on the website they can leave a phone number or e-mail.
3. You get an e-mail and a red **waiting** badge. Open the chat, type a reply and press **Send reply**.
4. Press **Hand back to assistant** when you're done. **Take over** pauses the assistant in any chat.

## Good to know

- Handoff is free. While a person is handling a chat, the assistant stays quiet and uses no credits.
- You get at most one e-mail per conversation every 15 minutes. If nobody replies for 24 hours, the chat goes back to
  the assistant. Both times can be changed by the platform.
- The inbox shows the 100 most recent conversations, with customers waiting for a reply first. It updates on its own.
- Phone-call conversations show the caller's number to call back (replies can't be typed into a phone call).
- Bot answers can be corrected from the transcript with [Fix this answer](qa-answers.md).

## 👍 / 👎 on answers

- Website visitors can rate each answer. Clicking the same button again removes the rating.
- Each conversation in the inbox shows how many answers were rated 👎.
- In [Analytics](analytics.md), answers marked 👎 are grouped by question, each with a **Fix this answer** button.

Platform settings for handoff (e-mail, timings) are in [human handoff](../handoff.md).
