# Plans and billing

**Where:** **Plans & billing** in the dashboard

| Plan | Price / month | Answers / month | Agents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | 50 | 1 |
| Starter | ₹999 | 1,000 | 3 |
| Growth | ₹2,999 | 5,000 | 10 |
| Pro | ₹7,999 | 20,000 | 30 |

- One **message credit** is one AI answer: on the website, by voice, in the playground or on WhatsApp. Handoffs, your own
  replies and voice-note transcription use no credits.
- **Upgrading** goes through Razorpay Checkout (cards, UPI Autopay). Your plan changes only when Razorpay confirms the
  payment. **Cancelling** keeps the paid plan until the end of the billing period. To switch plans, cancel first.
- The platform's team can also give your account bonus credits for a month.

## Your account

- Sign up and log in with e-mail and password. Each account sees only its own agents and data.
- **Forgot password?** On the login page, enter your e-mail to get a link that works once, for 60 minutes. Setting a new
  password signs out every other device.

The prices are placeholders the platform sets in `packages/core/src/plans.ts`. Setting up Razorpay is covered in
[Razorpay billing](../billing-razorpay.md).
